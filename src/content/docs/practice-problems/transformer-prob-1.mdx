## Overview

Compute scaled dot-product attention scores between query and key vectors. Scaling dot products by the square root of the head dimension prevents attention logits from growing excessively large in high dimensions, keeping softmax gradients stable during training.

## Task

Implement `attention_score(q, k)`: compute `dot(q, k) / sqrt(len(q))` where `q` and `k` are float vectors of equal length. Return the resulting float rounded to 4 decimal places. If `q` and `k` have differing lengths or are empty, raise `ValueError`.
