[Level-Order Traversal](/practice/level-order-traversal) flattened an entire tree into one list, level by level. This variant needs BFS to be **level-aware**, not just level-*ordered*: imagine standing to the right of the tree — what's the sequence of nodes actually visible? For each level, that's exactly the *last* node BFS would visit at that depth, and the plain flat-queue approach from Level-Order Traversal has no notion of "where one level ends and the next begins" to make that possible.

**Your task**: implement `right_side_view(root)`, returning a list of one value per level (the rightmost node's value), top to bottom.
