$$\text{RMSNorm}(x)_i = \frac{x_i}{\sqrt{\frac{1}{n}\sum_j x_j^2 + \epsilon}} \cdot \gamma_i$$

RMSNorm is a simplified LayerNorm: instead of subtracting the mean *and* dividing by the standard deviation, it only rescales by the root-mean-square of the activations — no mean-centering, no learned bias term. That's cheaper to compute with comparable training stability, which is exactly why it's the current default normalization in most modern LLMs (LLaMA and its successors) instead of the older LayerNorm.
