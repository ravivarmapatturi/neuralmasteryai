**Difficulty: Hard** · Topic: Deep Learning · Concept: [Backpropagation & Autograd](/docs/deep-learning/neural-network-fundamentals)

Automatic differentiation is the core mechanism enabling neural network training. In this challenge, you will implement a scalar autograd engine that tracks forward mathematical operations and executes topological reverse-mode backpropagation to compute analytical gradients.
