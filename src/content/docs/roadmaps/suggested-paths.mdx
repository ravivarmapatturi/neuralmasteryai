# Suggested Paths

[The Neural Mastery Roadmap](/docs/roadmaps/overview) is the complete, zero-to-job-ready path through every section. This page is different: it's a single, flat, **priority-ranked list of the specific skills that show up most often in real high-paying ML/AI/GenAI job postings** — not a tour of this site's sections, a direct answer to "what do I actually need to know to crack this role, in order of how much it matters."

The ranking isn't a guess. It's built from a real sample of 17 current job postings — 7 India-market AI-engineer roles (₹30–85 LPA) and 10 US roles sourced from LinkedIn ($141K–$400K/yr, including Genentech, EarnIn, and several well-funded applied-AI startups) — counting how many of the 17 actually asked for each skill. **Must-Learn** = asked for in at least half the sample (9+ of 17). **High Priority** = asked for by 4–8, a real ceiling-raiser. **Niche** = asked for by 3 or fewer, a real differentiator when the specific role calls for it. Work top to bottom; by the time you're through Must-Learn you can already hold your own in most interviews for this role.

## Tier 1 — Must-Learn

The skills that showed up in at least half the real postings sampled. If you only have time for one tier, this is it.

1. **Multi-agent systems & agent architectures** (14 of 17 postings) — [Agent Architectures](/docs/agents/agent-architectures), [Agents Roadmap](/docs/agents/roadmap). By far the single most-requested skill in the whole sample — production multi-agent systems, not agent demos.
2. **Prototype → production ownership** (13 of 17) — [ML System Design Roadmap](/docs/ml-system-design/roadmap), [ReAct Agent in Production](/docs/agents/react-agent-production). Not "can you build a demo" — can you take it the rest of the way: reliability, monitoring, the unglamorous 80%.
3. **Ownership, startup / 0→1 execution, mentoring** (13 of 17) — the trait side of #2, and it's not a page you read. It's demonstrated in the interview itself: have a specific story ready where you owned a messy, ambiguous problem end-to-end — the decision-making and tradeoffs, not just the code — and, if relevant, a time you unblocked or mentored someone else.
4. **Eval harnesses — LLM-as-judge, regression testing, golden datasets** (11 of 17) — [LLM, RAG & Agent Evaluation](/docs/ai-evaluation/llm-rag-agent-evaluation). This jumped to Must-Learn with the US sample added — nearly every serious AI team now treats "how do you know it's actually working" as a first-class engineering question, not an afterthought.
5. **RAG pipelines — chunking, hybrid search, reranking** (10 of 17) — [RAG](/docs/llms-genai/rag). Still the backbone of most real GenAI products.
6. **Production Python — async, Pydantic, concurrency** (12 of 17) — [Python Engineering Roadmap](/docs/python-engineering/roadmap). Not "I know Python" — specifically the patterns behind every FastAPI-backed AI service's codebase.

## Tier 2 — High Priority

Each of these showed up in 4–8 of the 17 postings — not universal, but a real, specific edge once Tier 1 is solid.

7. **Tool use, planning, orchestration, agent state & routing** (8) — [ReAct Agent in Production](/docs/agents/react-agent-production).
8. **Cloud deployment at scale — AWS / GCP / Azure** (8) — [Cloud Computing](/docs/mlops/cloud-computing), [Cloud Model Deployment](/docs/mlops/cloud-model-deployment).
9. **Data pipelines & distributed, large-scale data** (8) — [Data Engineering & Versioning](/docs/mlops/data-engineering-and-versioning), [Pipeline Orchestration](/docs/mlops/pipeline-orchestration).
10. **Product judgment — working directly with users/customers** (8) — a real, recurring ask across the US sample specifically; demonstrated by talking concretely about *why* you built something a certain way for the actual user, not just that you built it.
11. **Backend APIs — FastAPI, gRPC, microservices** (7) — [Model Serving](/docs/mlops/model-serving), [APIs, HTTP & Protocols](/docs/cs-fundamentals/apis-http-and-protocols).

15. **System design & testing fundamentals** (6) — [ML System Design Roadmap](/docs/ml-system-design/roadmap).
16. **Error analysis, A/B tests, experimentation** (6) — [AI Evaluation Roadmap](/docs/ai-evaluation/roadmap).
17. **CI/CD, MLOps, Git** (6) — [CI/CD & ML CI/CD](/docs/mlops/cicd-and-ml-cicd).
18. **LLM APIs — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google** (6) — [LLMs & GenAI Roadmap](/docs/llms-genai/roadmap).

## Tier 3 — Niche

Each of these was asked for by 3 or fewer of the 17 postings — don't front-load these, but a real premium when the specific role calls for it.

24. **Embeddings & vector DBs — Pinecone, Weaviate, Milvus** (3) — [Vector Databases](/docs/databases/vector/overview).
25. **PyTorch / TensorFlow, classical ML, NLP** (3) — [Machine Learning Roadmap](/docs/machine-learning/roadmap). Lower here than you'd expect — the sample skews heavily toward applied/agentic roles over classical modeling.
26. **SQL, Postgres, Redis — data modeling** (3) — [PostgreSQL](/docs/databases/relational/postgresql).
27. **Structured outputs / structured generation** (3) — [LLMs & GenAI Roadmap](/docs/llms-genai/roadmap).
28. **LangGraph / LangChain** (2) — [Plan-and-Execute Agents](/docs/agents/plan-and-execute).
29. **AI coding tools — Claude Code, Cursor, Codex** (2) — [Agentic Coding Assistants](/docs/agents/agentic-coding-assistants). Newly real, but worth knowing: several teams explicitly ask whether you already build with these day-to-day.
30. **MCP (Model Context Protocol)** (2) — [MCP Overview](/docs/agents/mcp/overview).
31. **Workflow engines — Temporal, Celery, async task graphs** (2) — [Agents Roadmap](/docs/agents/roadmap).
32. **VPC / on-prem / edge deployment** (2) — [Cloud Model Deployment](/docs/mlops/cloud-model-deployment).
33. **Research paper → product implementation** (2) — [Research Engineering Roadmap](/docs/research-engineering/roadmap).
34. **Agent SDKs — Claude Agent SDK, OpenAI Agents SDK** (1) — [Multi-Agent Systems](/docs/agents/multi-agent-systems).
35. **Agent memory, skills, long-horizon planning** (1) — [Multi-Agent Systems](/docs/agents/multi-agent-systems).
36. **Post-training — DPO, RLHF, preference optimization** (1) — [Training Pipeline](/docs/llms-genai/training-pipeline).
37. **Transformer fundamentals — GPT, BERT** (1) — [Attention & Transformers](/docs/deep-learning/attention-transformers).
38. **Knowledge graphs / ontologies** (1) — [Graph Databases](/docs/databases/graph/overview).
39. **Speech & audio — ASR/TTS, distributed GPU training** (1) — [Speech & Audio Roadmap](/docs/speech-audio/roadmap).
